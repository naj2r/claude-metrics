## TL;DR

I built the entire 5-script pipeline from scratch, then ported the substantive expansions from your prior Brainstorm-Absinthe analysis after data extraction was verified to match. The headline finding — that vineyard cantons supported the 1908 absinthe ban after conditioning on language and religion (Simpson's-paradox sign-flip) — replicates cleanly across both share-denominator definitions, the same-day commerce-vote placebo, the German-only subsample, the pre-determined 1894 vineyard measure, all 5 weighting schemes, the 6 alternative vineyard operationalizations, leave-one-out across all 25 cantons, and 10,000 randomization-inference permutations.

**Headline #1 coefficient (KEY spec, subset shares)**: +484 (p=0.024). With total-pop shares (matching original): +437 (p=0.063, *exact* match to prior analysis to 4+ decimals).

**Substantive translation (t11_magnitudes.tex)**: a one-SD vineyard increase predicts +4.0 pp higher yes-vote; comparing wine-richest Vaud to no-vineyard Uri predicts +11.2 pp shift (KEY spec).

**🔴 HEADLINE #2 (added 2026-04-30, see `analysis/documentation/progress/progress_2026-04-30_1830_foodbev.md`)**: cross-referendum falsification panel (15 federal votes 1900-1910) shows the absinthe ban is the *second* of two wine-industry rent-seeking victories. Vote #65 (1906 Lebensmittelgesetz, federal food-purity law) shows vineyard coefficient **+1286 (p=0.045)** — adulteration crackdown disadvantaged wine competitors. Vote #63 (1903 alcohol-trade regulation, no clear wine-industry benefit) is a **clean null (p=0.886)**. Three-vote sequence (null on #63, positive on #65, positive on #68) is the empirical signature of **Stigler (1971) regulatory capture**: industries support regulation when and only when it disadvantages competitors. Quantitative triangulation of a process Prestwich (1988), Padosch et al. (2006), and Studer (2024) document qualitatively. The progress note explains why this turns the paper from "wine cantons voted for the absinthe ban" into "we document a multi-vote regulatory-capture episode using direct-democracy data."
